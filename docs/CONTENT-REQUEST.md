# SGD Electric — content required from the client

Everything on the site today is placeholder written during the build. This is the
full list of what has to be replaced before launch, in the order it should be
chased.

**Scale of the job:** 239 content records, 64 stock photographs, 6 stock videos,
26 legal sections, across 23 pages.

---

## Tier 1 — Legal. The site cannot go live without these.

These carry real risk, not just polish. Every one of them was invented.

### 1.1 Company identity
- [ ] Registered legal entity name (currently *"SGD Electric Mobility Pvt. Ltd."*)
- [ ] CIN / company registration number
- [ ] GSTIN
- [ ] Registered office address (must match MCA records)
- [ ] Year founded (currently *2019*)

### 1.2 Policies — must come from their lawyer, not from us
- [ ] **Privacy Policy** — 10 sections currently invented
- [ ] **Terms of Service** — 10 sections currently invented
- [ ] **Cookie Policy** — 6 sections currently invented
- [ ] **Grievance Officer** name, email and postal address — legally mandatory
      in India under the DPDP Act 2023 and the IT Rules. Not on the site at all
      right now.
- [ ] Refund / cancellation policy (we quote a ₹3,000 refundable deposit)
- [ ] Data retention periods, and which sub-processors they use

> The app flow we designed collects **Aadhaar, PAN, a selfie and bank details**.
> That is sensitive personal data. The privacy policy has to name the legal basis,
> retention period and grievance route, and Aadhaar verification can only be done
> through a licensed KUA/AUA partner. Ask who that partner is.

### 1.3 Claims that need substantiation
Indian advertising rules (ASCI) require evidence for these. All are invented:
- [ ] "₹9,400 saved per rider per month"
- [ ] "68M kg CO₂ avoided" — and the methodology behind it
- [ ] "132M+ deliveries", "24,000 riders", "12 cities"
- [ ] **"No licence required"** — a regulatory claim about sub-25 km/h vehicles.
      Needs compliance sign-off in writing.
- [ ] "₹5 lakh accident cover" — needs the actual insurer name and policy number
- [ ] "45 minute roadside response", "90 second battery swap"

### 1.4 Third-party trademarks — currently a live risk
The homepage shows **Swiggy, Zomato, Blinkit, Zepto, Flipkart, Amazon,
BigBasket, Dunzo, Delhivery, Instamart, Licious and Rapido** as clients, and the
investors section names eight funds.

- [ ] Written permission for every brand shown as a client or partner, **or**
      a list of the ones we may actually name, **or** we remove the section.

> The partner names are currently rendered as styled text rather than real logos,
> which lowers the risk but does not remove it — it still asserts a commercial
> relationship. The investor names in the file are deliberately fictional and
> must not be published as-is.

## How to send it

Easiest for everyone:

1. One spreadsheet, **one tab per section above**, with a row per record.
2. Images in a shared drive folder, foldered by the same section names, original
   resolution, not compressed and not pasted into a document.
3. Legal policies as separate documents from their lawyer.
4. A single named contact who can confirm figures and approve claims.

Anything not supplied stays as a placeholder, and every placeholder is a thing
that has to be corrected after launch instead of before it.
